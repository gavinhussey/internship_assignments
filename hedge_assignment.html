Formula & Variables

Optimal Hedge Formula
N* = h* × (VP ÷ VF)     where   VF = F × Q   and   h* = ρ × (σS ÷ σF)
SymbolMeaning
N*Number of futures contracts to sell short
VPPortfolio value ($10,000,000)
VFNotional value per contract (F × Q)
FLive futures price
QContract size / multiplier
h*Optimal hedge ratio (ρ × σS ÷ σF)
ρCorrelation between spot & futures price changes
σSVolatility of spot price changes
σFVolatility of futures price changes
βPPortfolio beta (equities only, via OLS regression)
